# Establish purpose and constraints

Use before deep research, and again when a new stakeholder joins the project.
The answers become the evaluation criteria for the research and the report;
they do not authorize production, publication or source access.

## Read what already exists first

Read the material the user supplied or pointed to: meeting notes, proposals,
filled source checklists, previous PLANs and the existing workspace's guidance.
Extract the stated goals, commitments, open questions and the action items
assigned to each person. When a meeting record is a transcript without a summary,
derive the decisions and action items from the conversation and label them as
derived. Ask only about what remains unknown.

## Questions to settle

Ask the missing ones together, in one exchange, in the conversation language:

| Topic | What to learn | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing knowledge | Where knowledge already lives (repositories, wikis, documents, packages) and who maintains each | Determines the inventory and who to ask when access fails |
| Maintenance afterwards | Whether each collection keeps being maintained where it is, or moves into the workspace | Decides between following an upstream and absorbing it once |
| Content that must not change | Procedures, policies or pages that must keep their original wording | Decides whether an unchanged or reference-only approach is needed |
| Readers and uses | Who reads the result: coding agents, Q&A, on-call, onboarding | Shapes article boundaries and package split |
| Package split | Whether different readers need separate packages, and their scope | Decides package selection, independent of navigation |
| Structural constraints | Limits such as keeping the existing top-level sections | Constrains where new topics may go |
| Delivery and review | Review mode, reporting rhythm, delivery destination | Inherited by the PLAN and its stages |

Accept "not decided yet" as an answer and record it as an open decision; do
not block research on it. Do not ask about permissions the user cannot grant.

## Record and reuse

Write the answers into the PLAN's purpose and decision sections, separating
facts the user stated, items derived from documents and open questions. Reuse
them across stages and resumed sessions; ask again only when the scope changes
materially or a stakeholder contradicts an earlier answer.
